About

Engineer, leader,
maker.

I'm a mechatronics engineer based in Knoxville, Tennessee. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design, embedded electronics, and the software that connects them. to be.

I've spent the past several years doing R&D for medical device and life science clients through my consulting practice, taking products from early concept through working prototype. That means I'm comfortable with ambiguity, tight timelines, and the kind of problem-solving that happens when a design meets reality on the bench.

On the tools side: I design in SolidWorks, simulate in OpenFOAM and ANSYS, write firmware for ESP32 and Arduino targets, and do most of my data work in Python. I'm also comfortable laying out custom PCBs and have a decent soldering station to show for it.

Outside of engineering I spend time woodturning, baking sourdough, and keeping up a small electronics lab at home. I'm currently looking for full-time mechatronics or systems engineering roles where I can build things that matter.
